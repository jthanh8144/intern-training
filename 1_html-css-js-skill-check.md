### Reading section subtotals

Because each section has exactly 15 questions, subtotals are directly comparable. As a rule of thumb:

- **12–15 correct** in a section: strong grasp of that topic.
- **8–11 correct**: workable but uneven — review specific missed questions.
- **0–7 correct**: that topic needs focused, structured study before relying on it in real work.

A learner who scores 'Proficient' or higher overall but is weak in one section (e.g. strong HTML/CSS, weak JS) should prioritize that section rather than repeating the whole assessment.
